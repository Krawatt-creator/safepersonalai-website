# Logo generation prompts — SafePersonalAI

Style notes that apply to all 5 (repeat in the tool if it accepts a global
style): flat vector mark, not a 3D render or photo; simple enough to read
clearly at 16×16px (favicon size); works as a standalone mark AND
alongside the wordmark "SafePersonalAI"; primary color `#00e599` (a clean
mint-green), secondary `#8e55ea` (violet) used sparingly if at all,
everything else near-black/white; explicitly avoid: robots, glowing
brains, neural-network/circuit-board patterns, padlocks or shields as the
main shape, 3D glass orbs, purple-blue gradient blobs, generic "AI
sparkle" icons. Think Apple's own app icons or Linear's mark — restrained,
geometric, one clear idea — not a generic tech-startup logo.

---

### 1 — The Boundary Mark
A perfect circle cleanly divided by one straight vertical line down the
middle: the left half a solid dark charcoal, the right half solid mint
green (#00e599), no gradient, hard edge between them, no other detail.
The mark should read instantly as "two sides, one clean line" — literally
the brand's core idea (AI's side, your side). Flat, geometric, vector,
centered on a transparent or white background, square canvas.

### 2 — The Quiet Dot
A single small solid mint-green circle, perfectly centered, with one thin
(1.5–2px equivalent) charcoal ring around it at a modest distance — like
a status light with a calm halo, not a target or radar. No gradient, no
glow effect, no rays. Should feel like "one quiet signal," restrained and
still, not busy or radiant. Flat vector, square canvas, transparent
background.

### 3 — The Approval Check
A rounded square (app-icon proportions, like an iOS icon shape) in dark
charcoal, with a single simple checkmark stroke in mint green centered
inside it — the checkmark drawn as one continuous, slightly rounded
stroke, not a bold slab. No circle behind the checkmark, no additional
ornamentation. Should read as calm confirmation, not an alert or badge.
Flat vector, square canvas.

### 4 — The Open Gate
Two simple vertical bars (like a minimal gate or open parenthesis-shaped
pair) standing slightly apart, dark charcoal, with a small solid mint
dot passing between the gap. Suggests "something passes through only
when there's a clear way" without literally drawing a lock, key, or
shield. Extremely minimal, no more than 3 shapes total. Flat vector,
square canvas, transparent background.

### 5 — The Monogram
A custom geometric monogram built from the letters "S" and "P" (for
SafePersonalAI) merged into one simple continuous line-based glyph, dark
charcoal, with exactly one small mint-green accent point where the two
letterforms meet or cross. Should look like it belongs next to the
wordmark "SafePersonalAI" in a nav bar — restrained, geometric,
lowercase-adjacent proportions, no serifs, no 3D bevels. Flat vector,
square canvas.
