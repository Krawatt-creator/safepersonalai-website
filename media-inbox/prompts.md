# Video generation prompts — SafePersonalAI homepage

Style notes that apply to all 10 (repeat these in the tool if it lets you
set a global style): shot on a real camera, not CGI or 3D-rendered; natural
window/lamp light, soft shadows, shallow depth of field; calm, unhurried
pacing — no fast cuts, no dramatic music sting; no glowing holograms, no
robots, no neural-network/circuit-board graphics, no purple/blue sci-fi
gradients, no on-screen text or logos (added later in the edit). Think
Apple's own product films, not a generic "AI startup" ad.

---

### 1 — `01-hero-loop.mp4` — Hero background, ambient loop
A closed MacBook sits on a minimalist wood desk in a softly lit room,
morning light coming through a window just out of frame. Slow, smooth
push-in on the laptop. Nothing else moves except dust catching the light.
Loopable — first and last frame should match closely. 10–15 seconds,
16:9, no people, no hands.

### 2 — `02-voice-to-calendar.mp4` — Use Cases: voice message → appointment
A person walking outside, coat on, glances at their phone and taps once to
record a quick voice memo, then puts the phone back in their pocket without
breaking stride. Casual, unposed, natural outdoor light. No dialogue needed
— the phone screen isn't the focus, the gesture is. 8–10 seconds, vertical
or 16:9.

### 3 — `03-invoice-to-payment.mp4` — Use Cases: invoice photo → tracked payment
Close-up, top-down: a hand holding a phone photographs a paper utility
bill sitting on a kitchen counter next to a coffee mug. The phone screen
shows a normal camera viewfinder, nothing futuristic. Warm, everyday
lighting. 6–8 seconds, 16:9 or square.

### 4 — `04-email-to-reply.mp4` — Use Cases: email → drafted reply
A person on a couch in the evening, phone in hand, reading something with
a small relaxed smile, then setting the phone face-down on the cushion and
picking up a cup of tea instead. Warm lamp light, cozy, unhurried. 8–10
seconds, vertical or 16:9.

### 5 — `05-travel-alert.mp4` — Travel module teaser
A person at an airport gate or train platform, glancing at their phone,
a small satisfied nod, then looking back up and out toward the window/
platform. Natural travel-day energy but calm, not rushed. 6–8 seconds,
16:9.

### 6 — `06-wealth-reconciliation.mp4` — Wealth module teaser
Overhead shot: a small stack of paper bills and a calculator on a desk at
the start, hands moving them aside, then the same hands setting a phone
down showing a clean, simple, organized-looking (but not readable-text)
screen next to the now-cleared desk. Tidy, satisfying, minimal. 8–10
seconds, top-down, square or 16:9.

### 7 — `07-the-boundary.mp4` — Boundary section, brand moment
Two-part gesture, same continuous shot: a hand hovers over a laptop
trackpad or a phone screen, pauses (a full beat of stillness — this pause
is the point), then deliberately presses down once, calm and intentional,
like signing something rather than tapping through a notification. No
urgency. This shot should read as "a deliberate decision," not "clicking
through an app." 5–7 seconds, close-up on the hand, shallow depth of
field.

### 8 — `08-morning-wake.mp4` — Possible ad intro
A bedroom or home office at first light. A closed MacBook on a desk;
sunlight slowly brightens across the room over the shot (time-lapse feel
but still smooth, not jittery). Right at the end, the very corner of the
laptop's status light (if visible) or the screen edge gives one soft,
brief glow — subtle, easy to miss, not a dramatic reveal. 8–12 seconds,
16:9.

### 9 — `09-evening-close.mp4` — Possible ad outro
Reverse mood of #8: dusk light through a window, a person closing their
MacBook lid gently at the end of a workday, the room dimming as the
screen's light fades out last. Quiet, contented, "done for the day"
feeling. 6–8 seconds, 16:9.

### 10 — `10-quiet-trust.mp4` — Trust section texture
A phone lying face-up on a table buzzes once with a subtle, non-intrusive
notification glow; a hand picks it up, glances briefly, sets it back down
face-down, and returns to what they were doing (stirring coffee, petting a
dog, reading a book — pick one). The point is how UNDRAMATIC it is. 8–10
seconds, 16:9.
